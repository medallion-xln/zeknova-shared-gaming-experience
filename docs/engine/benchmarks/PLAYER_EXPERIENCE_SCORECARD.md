The boss results are scoped fixture-assisted certification: four prerequisite Solar Grids, survey visits, resources and initial position were seeded in isolated local accounts. The final fifth building and full 818 HP fight used physical controls. Finishing the final objective in the intended Desert biome and separately in Highlands both automatically staged Sunscar in Desert; victories took 49.818/44.935 seconds, with 2/3 deaths and one retained reward claim each. Mission 1 completion and Mission 2 unlock are verified; all 12 missions, all bosses and a natural full campaign are not.

The initial “save blocked by modal” finding is not counted as a failure to close an expected decision UI. The harness left a diplomatic incident unanswered. Its resulting pointer timeouts limit the 15-minute play claim. The actual product issue is that background keyboard movement/combat continues while that modal owns pointer interaction. A later controlled outbox retest recovered in 36.342 seconds and real 401 authentication denial preserved pending state; there is no demonstrated irreversible save loss.

## Prioritized decisions

- **Continued development: GO.** The original game has functioning movement/combat/harvest, a fixture-certified first mission/boss/reward path, physical construction validation and acknowledged/recoverable saves. The source SDK and durable engine offer a useful bounded foundation.
- **Professional gameplay quality: NO-GO.** Fix the source-world continuous-input stall, original construction layout failure and modal input isolation, then complete natural progression/rank/world coverage and actual human onboarding/audio/fun testing.
- **Live authority transfer: NO-GO.** The legacy writer remains authoritative; the independent construction world does not close all live gameplay/effect/writer migration gates.
- **Production release: NO-GO.** Original build/golden lineage, authority parity, production identity/TLS/operations and owner security/release gates remain unresolved. No production authorization is inferred from local staging evidence.

The source-world input failure is included in the bounded multiplayer assessment. Studio final evidence updates only its scoped rating and the explicitly limited weighted-score eligibility. The provisional 6/10 game assessment is not a claim that missing campaign, audio or fun gates passed.
