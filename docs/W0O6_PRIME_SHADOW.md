# W0-O.6 — Sentinel Prime Shadow Integration

W0-O.6 connects Sentinel Prime to the canonical W0-O.3 592-feature faction observation and the W0-O.5 six-action legal mask.

The implementation is shadow-only. Prime may rank legal actions, select a canonical legal target for telemetry, compare its recommendation with an explicitly projected incumbent decision, and append deterministic records to a private shadow ledger. It cannot execute, commit, dispatch, or apply an action.

The current engine is a deterministic root-only masked PUCT development scaffold. It is not a trained policy and does not claim long-horizon world control. The incumbent scripted AI remains authoritative for live behavior.
